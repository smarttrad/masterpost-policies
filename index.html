MasterPost Policies

MasterPost is a local social management application for accounts owned or administered by authorized operators. It supports Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Threads profiles. A YouTube video publishing adapter has been implemented locally and requires Google authorization and live verification before use. X/Twitter and TikTok remain connection-only.

Privacy, revocation and data-management safeguards for new providers must be completed before live use. These pages explain actual capabilities and provider-specific obligations; they do not certify approval by any platform.
