MasterPost Policies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

1. About MasterPost

MasterPost is a local social media publishing and management application. It is used by authorized operators to manage Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and Threads profiles that they own or administer. MasterPost is not offered as a public consumer application and does not provide public user accounts.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features enabled, MasterPost may process:

  • Facebook Page, Instagram and Threads account identifiers;
  • access tokens and authorization metadata required to use official Meta APIs;
  • content prepared for publication, media URLs, publishing times, post identifiers and publishing status;
  • public comments and related public profile identifiers when comment-management features are enabled; and
  • technical logs used for security, troubleshooting, duplicate prevention and delivery verification.

MasterPost does not request access to Facebook or Instagram private messages. It does not intentionally collect payment-card details, passwords or other private account credentials from members of the public.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized business accounts, publish and manage social content, prevent duplicate posts, monitor token health, process supported public-comment workflows, maintain security and diagnose technical problems.

4. Storage and service providers

Operational credentials and logs are stored on the local systems controlled by the authorized operator. Media prepared for social publishing may be stored through Cloudinary when that service is configured. Published content may contain links selected by the operator. Meta receives information necessary to provide the Facebook, Instagram and Threads APIs. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Sharing and sale of information

MasterPost and its operator do not sell personal information processed by the application. Information is not shared for unrelated advertising purposes. It may be disclosed to service providers only when necessary to operate the application, comply with law, protect security or respond to a valid legal request.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operation, security, legal compliance and troubleshooting. Access tokens are kept confidential and are removed, replaced or revoked when no longer required. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of information associated with you that is held by MasterPost. Instructions are available on the Data Deletion Instructions page. You may also remove the application's access through the relevant Meta account settings, where available.

8. Children's privacy

MasterPost is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MasterPost's functionality or legal obligations change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material revisions are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning MasterPost, contact 1affiliatesurfmart@gmail.com.
