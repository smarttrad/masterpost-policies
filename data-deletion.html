MasterPost Policies

Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: September 20, 2026

To request deletion of information associated with you that may have been processed by MasterPost, email 1affiliatesurfmart@gmail.com with the subject line MasterPost Data Deletion Request.

Information to include

  • Your name;
  • the Facebook, Instagram or Threads profile URL or username connected to the request;
  • a description of the information you want deleted; and
  • any relevant public post or comment URL.

Do not email passwords, access tokens, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other unnecessary sensitive information.

What happens next

Asian Instant Coffee will acknowledge the request and may ask for limited information needed to verify that the requester is authorized to make it. Verified information under MasterPost's control will be deleted or anonymized unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Where applicable, you may also remove MasterPost's authorization through your Facebook, Instagram or Threads account settings. Revoking authorization stops future access but may not automatically delete operational records already retained by Asian Instant Coffee, so an email request should still be submitted when deletion is required.

Response time

We aim to respond to verified deletion requests within 30 days, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.
